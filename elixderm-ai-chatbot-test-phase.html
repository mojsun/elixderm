Executive Summary

Before committing to a full-scale AI chatbot implementation, this test phase allows Elixderm to validate user interest and engagement with a functional chatbot system. Over a 30-day testing period, we'll deploy a simplified but fully operational chatbot to measure real user interaction rates and gather data to inform future development decisions.

This approach minimizes risk and investment while providing concrete data on whether your website visitors will engage with an AI assistant after submitting contact forms.

Important Note on Scope: This is not simply adding a feature to your existing website. We're developing a complete product from the ground up, which includes comprehensive product design, data architecture and modeling, AI behavior engineering, and system integration. This involves creating new database schemas, designing user experiences, engineering AI prompts and conversational flows, and building custom interfaces that work seamlessly with your existing platform.

Accelerated Development Schedule: We're prioritizing a fast-tracked 4-5 week development timeline to get the chatbot live quickly. This accelerated approach helps you begin collecting engagement data sooner, enabling faster business assessment and decision-making. The sooner you can test and evaluate user interaction, the sooner you can determine the ROI potential and decide on next steps with confidence.

Test Phase Objectives

  • Validate that users are interested in interacting with an AI chatbot after form submission
  • Measure engagement rate: what percentage of form submitters interact with the chatbot
  • Assess conversation quality and typical question patterns
  • Identify gaps in the knowledge base that need expansion
  • Evaluate impact on consultation call bookings
  • Gather user feedback on the chat experience
  • Determine ROI potential before full-scale investment

What This Test Phase Includes

Core Functionality

1. Post-Form Chat Activation

When a user submits the contact form, instead of just a "Thank you" message, a chat window immediately appears on the screen.

2. Context-Aware Personalization

The chatbot receives all information from the submitted form (name, company, product type, timeline, business stage, etc.) and uses this data to personalize the interaction.

3. Intelligent Greeting

The AI greets the user personally based on their form submission. For example:

"Hi Sarah! Thanks for your interest in developing your anti-aging serum line. I'm here to answer any questions you might have about our formulation process, timelines, or requirements. I'm trained on our most common inquiries and should be able to help. What would you like to know?"

4. Interactive Q&A Conversation

Users can type questions naturally, and the AI responds with accurate information drawn from a knowledge base of 30-50 frequently asked questions (to be provided by Elixderm team) covering:

  • Formulation development process and timeline
  • Minimum order quantities (MOQ)
  • Packaging options and design services
  • Testing requirements and regulatory compliance
  • Pricing structure and payment terms
  • Custom formulation capabilities
  • Shipping and logistics
  • Quality control and certifications

5. Smart Call-to-Action

After the user has asked a few questions (typically 3-5 exchanges), the AI naturally suggests booking a consultation call:

"It sounds like you have a clear vision for your product line. Would you like to schedule a 30-minute consultation call with our formulation team to discuss your project in detail? [Book a Call]"

6. Exit CTA

If a user closes the chat window without booking a call (and they've interacted with the bot), a final call-to-action modal appears:

"Before you go - would you like to schedule a call with our team to discuss your project? [Book a Call] [No Thanks]"

7. Complete Data Storage

Every chat conversation is automatically saved to your database, including:

  • Full transcript of all messages
  • Timestamp of conversation start and end
  • User information from contact form
  • Whether user clicked booking link
  • Number of messages exchanged
  • Conversation duration

8. Sanity Admin Dashboard Integration

View all chat conversations directly in your existing Sanity CMS admin panel:

  • Each contact form submission shows a "View Chat History" button
  • Click to see full conversation transcript in readable format
  • See engagement metrics (message count, duration, booking status)
  • Filter and search through conversations
  • Export chat data for analysis

What's NOT Included in Test Phase

To keep costs minimal for testing purposes, the following features are excluded from this phase:

  • Voice/audio conversation mode
  • User login and account system
  • AI-generated lead quality scoring and analysis
  • Advanced analytics dashboard
  • Suggested question predictions
  • Multi-session conversation history
  • Post-chat satisfaction survey

These features can be added in Phase 2 if the test phase shows strong user engagement.

Testing Metrics & Success Criteria

After 30 days of live testing, we'll analyze:

Metric What We'll Measure Good Benchmark
Engagement Rate Percentage of form submitters who interact with the chatbot 40%+ interaction rate
Conversation Depth Average number of messages per conversation 6+ messages (3+ exchanges)
Booking Conversion Percentage of chat users who click booking link 15%+ click-through rate
Question Coverage Percentage of questions AI can answer vs. "I don't know" 70%+ successful responses
Session Duration Average time users spend chatting 2+ minutes per session

Decision Point: If engagement rate exceeds 35% and users are asking meaningful questions that the AI can answer, this validates the concept and justifies investment in the full implementation with advanced features.

Technical Implementation Steps

Week 1: Foundation Setup

Database Schema Design

  • Create chatSession schema in Sanity for storing conversation metadata
  • Create chatMessage schema for individual messages
  • Link chat sessions to existing contact form submissions
  • Set up proper data relationships and indexing

OpenAI Integration

  • Configure OpenAI API credentials securely
  • Set up GPT-4o Mini model for cost-effective conversations
  • Implement streaming responses for real-time chat experience
  • Configure token limits and rate limiting

Knowledge Base Creation

  • Collaborative session with Elixderm team to document 30-50 FAQs (must be completed in Week 1)
  • Elixderm provides detailed, accurate answers for each question
  • Structure knowledge base in easily updatable format
  • Define AI personality and response guidelines

Note: Quick turnaround on FAQ content from your team is critical to maintaining the 4-5 week development timeline.

Week 2: Chat Interface Development

UI Components

  • Build chat window component (modern, responsive design)
  • Create message bubble display with proper formatting
  • Implement text input field with send functionality
  • Add typing indicators and loading states
  • Ensure mobile responsiveness

Contact Form Integration

  • Modify contact form submission handler to trigger chat window
  • Pass form data to chat system for personalization
  • Create unique session ID for each conversation
  • Implement chat launch animation

Conversation Logic

  • Build personalized greeting generator based on form data
  • Implement message exchange flow (user → AI → user)
  • Set up conversation context management
  • Configure AI to stay on-topic (manufacturing-related questions)

Week 3: CTA & Data Storage

Smart Call-to-Action System

  • Implement message counter to trigger booking suggestion
  • Create in-chat booking link display
  • Build exit modal with final CTA (only for engaged users)
  • Track booking link clicks

Database Storage

  • Implement automatic message saving to Sanity
  • Store conversation metadata (duration, message count, etc.)
  • Link conversations to contact submissions
  • Ensure data integrity and error handling

Sanity Admin Integration

  • Create custom Sanity component for chat viewing
  • Add "View Chat History" button to contact submissions
  • Build readable transcript display interface
  • Show engagement metrics alongside transcripts
  • Implement search and filter functionality

Week 4: Testing, Refinement & Launch

Quality Assurance

  • Test conversation flows with various question types
  • Verify AI response accuracy and helpfulness
  • Test on multiple browsers and devices
  • Ensure data is properly stored and displayed
  • Test edge cases (very long messages, rapid sending, etc.)

FAQ Refinement

  • Review AI responses for accuracy and tone
  • Refine knowledge base based on test conversations
  • Adjust AI prompts for better responses
  • Test fallback responses for unknown questions

Deployment & Training

  • Deploy to production environment
  • Configure monitoring and error tracking
  • Train Elixderm team on viewing chat data (1-hour session)
  • Set up analytics tracking for metrics
  • Go live and begin 30-day test period

Post-Test Scenarios

Scenario 1: Strong Engagement (Recommended Path)

If 35%+ of users interact with the chatbot and metrics show positive results:

  • Keep the basic chatbot running
  • Proceed to Phase 2 with advanced features (lead scoring, voice mode, suggested questions, etc.)
  • Invest in full implementation knowing it will deliver ROI
  • Expand knowledge base based on test insights

Scenario 2: Moderate Engagement

If 15-35% of users interact:

  • Analyze why engagement is lower than hoped
  • Consider UI/UX adjustments or timing changes
  • Test with improved approach for another 30 days
  • Make targeted improvements before full expansion

Scenario 3: Low Engagement

If under 15% of users interact:

  • Evaluate whether chatbot is the right solution
  • Consider alternative engagement strategies
  • Minimal investment means minimal loss
  • Data informs better decision-making for future initiatives
