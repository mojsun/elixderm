Phase 2 Development Proposal

January 31, 2026

Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the development of an intelligent AI chatbot system for Elixderm. The system will automatically engage visitors after they submit a contact form, answer their questions in real-time, and provide your team with detailed lead intelligence and quality scoring.

The AI assistant will serve as a 24/7 first point of contact, handling common questions, gauging customer interest and seriousness, and directing high-quality leads to book consultations with your team.

Project Options

Option 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP) - Recommended

What You Get:

  • Intelligent Text Chat Interface - After a visitor submits your contact form, they're immediately presented with a chat window where they can ask questions and get instant answers about your manufacturing services, capabilities, timelines, and processes.
  • Context-Aware Conversations - The AI already knows who they are (name, company, product type, timeline) from the contact form they just filled out, so conversations feel personal and relevant from the first message.
  • Smart Knowledge Base - The AI is trained on 30-50 of your most frequently asked questions and can provide accurate, helpful answers about formulation development, packaging options, minimum order quantities, testing requirements, regulatory compliance, and more.
  • Intelligent Lead Qualification - As the conversation progresses, the AI evaluates lead quality based on their questions, responses, and engagement level. It identifies serious buyers versus casual browsers.
  • Strategic Call-to-Action - When the AI detects a high-quality lead or encounters questions beyond its knowledge, it naturally suggests booking a consultation call with your team, displaying a clear booking link.
  • Complete Conversation Storage - Every chat is automatically saved to your Sanity CMS database, including full transcripts, timestamps, and all context from the original contact form.
  • Admin Dashboard Integration - Inside your existing Sanity admin panel, you'll see a new "Chat History" section for each contact form submission. Click any submission to view the full conversation transcript in an easy-to-read format.
  • AI-Generated Lead Analysis - After each conversation, a second AI analyzes the entire chat and generates a structured summary showing:
    • Lead Quality Score (1-10)
    • Seriousness/Readiness Level (Researching, Considering, Ready to Buy)
    • Main Topics Discussed (e.g., "packaging options, MOQ, timeline")
    • Concerns or Objections Raised
    • Recommended Next Steps
  • Post-Chat Feedback Survey - When the chat ends, users are asked to rate their experience (1-5 stars) and optionally provide comments. This feedback is stored and visible in your admin dashboard.
  • Handles Irrelevant Questions Gracefully - If someone asks about topics unrelated to manufacturing (weather, sports, etc.), the AI politely redirects them: "I specialize in helping with cosmetic manufacturing questions. I can help you with formulation development, packaging, testing, or regulatory compliance. What would you like to know?"
How It Works for Users:
  1. User fills out contact form and clicks Submit
  2. Instead of just a "Thank you" message, a chat window appears
  3. AI greets them by name: "Hi Sarah! Thanks for your interest in developing your serum line. I'm here to answer any questions while our team reviews your submission. What would you like to know?"
  4. Based on the contact form data and user's specific needs, the AI immediately predicts and displays 4 relevant questions they might want to ask (e.g., "What's the typical timeline for serum development?", "What are the minimum order quantities?", "Do you help with packaging design?", "What testing is required?"). User can click any question to start the conversation instantly, or type their own question.
  5. User chats naturally, asking follow-up questions
  6. AI provides helpful answers, asks clarifying questions when needed
  7. If lead seems high-quality or AI can't answer, it offers: "This sounds like a great fit! Would you like to schedule a 30-minute consultation call with our formulation team?"
  8. User rates the experience and either books a call or closes the chat

Option 2: Expanded Implementation

Everything in MVP, Plus:

  • Voice Conversation Mode - Users can click a microphone button and speak their questions out loud instead of typing. The AI responds with natural-sounding voice, creating a phone-call-like experience right in the browser. Particularly useful for busy entrepreneurs who prefer talking over typing. Uses OpenAI's Realtime Voice API for natural, human-like conversations with minimal latency.
  • User Account System - After the initial chat, the system can automatically create an account for users using their name and email from the contact form, or allow them to manually create one with a password (or social login with Google/LinkedIn). Once they have an account, they can:
    • Return anytime to continue previous conversations
    • Access their complete chat history
    • Start new conversations without re-submitting contact forms
    • Save important information the AI shares
    • Track their inquiry status (New, In Discussion, Quote Received, etc.)
  • Persistent Chat History - All conversations are organized by date, searchable, and accessible anytime. Users can reference what was discussed weeks ago, and the AI can recall context from previous conversations: "Last time we discussed your anti-aging serum formula. Have you decided on the packaging yet?"
  • Multi-Session Context - The AI maintains context across multiple conversations, so returning users don't need to repeat information. It builds on previous discussions naturally.
  • Notification System - Users receive email notifications when:
    • Your team responds to their inquiry
    • You have questions for them
    • Their project status changes
  • Enhanced Admin Dashboard - Your team sees which users are most engaged, how many conversations they've had, their cumulative engagement score across all sessions, and can flag specific users for priority follow-up.
Expanded User Experience:

After initial contact form and chat, the system can automatically create an account for the user (using their email and name from the form) and send them login credentials, or allow them to manually set up their own account. Two weeks later, they log back in and click "Continue Conversation." The AI greets them: "Welcome back, Sarah! Last time we discussed your serum line - you were deciding between 30ml and 50ml bottles. Did you have more questions, or are you ready to move forward?" The entire conversation history is available to review, and the AI remembers everything discussed previously.

Technical Implementation Plan

To deliver this system, we'll build and integrate multiple technical components. Here's exactly what needs to be done:

Phase A: Foundation & Infrastructure (Weeks 1-2)

1. Database Schema Design

Design and implement new data structures in Sanity CMS to store all chat-related information.

  • chatSession schema - Stores each chat session with unique ID, linked to the contact form submission, start/end times, and session metadata
  • chatMessage schema - Stores individual messages with sender (user or AI), timestamp, message content, and session reference
  • chatAnalysis schema - Stores the AI-generated lead analysis JSON with quality scores, topics, and recommendations
  • chatFeedback schema - Stores user satisfaction ratings and comments
  • userAccount schema - (Expanded only) Stores user credentials, profile info, and linked chat sessions

2. OpenAI API Integration

Set up secure connection to OpenAI's services for powering the AI conversations.

  • API Key Configuration - Securely store OpenAI API credentials in environment variables
  • GPT-4o Mini Chat Integration - Configure GPT-4o Mini model for text-based conversations with streaming support for real-time responses (cost-effective while maintaining quality)
  • Realtime Voice API Setup - (Expanded only) Integrate OpenAI's Realtime API for bidirectional voice conversations
  • Token Management - Implement usage tracking and rate limiting to control API costs

3. Context Management System

Build the system that feeds the AI relevant information about each user and conversation.

  • Contact Form Data Integration - Automatically pass user's name, company, product type, timeline, and all form fields to the AI at conversation start
  • FAQ Knowledge Base - Create a structured document containing 30-50 common questions and detailed answers that the AI will reference
  • System Prompts - Write and test the core AI instructions that define its personality, knowledge boundaries, and response style
  • Conversation Memory - Implement short-term memory so AI remembers the current conversation context
  • Multi-Session Context - (Expanded only) Implement long-term memory so AI recalls previous conversations with returning users

Phase B: Chat Interface Development (Weeks 3-4)

4. Chat UI Components

Build the user-facing chat interface that appears after form submission.

  • Chat Window Component - Modern, responsive chat interface with message bubbles, typing indicators, and smooth animations
  • Suggested Questions Display - After the AI greeting, display 4 AI-predicted relevant questions as clickable buttons based on the user's form data
  • Message Display - Properly formatted display of text messages with timestamps, sender labels, and Markdown support for formatted responses
  • Input Interface - Text input field with send button, character counter, and "Enter to send" functionality
  • Typing Indicators - Visual feedback showing "AI is thinking..." while waiting for responses
  • Voice Interface Components - (Expanded only) Microphone button, audio visualization, recording indicators, and voice playback controls
  • Mobile Responsiveness - Ensure chat works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops

5. Real-Time Communication

Implement the technology that enables instant back-and-forth messaging.

  • API Route Setup - Create Next.js API endpoints for sending/receiving messages
  • Streaming Responses - Implement server-sent events or WebSocket for real-time message streaming (words appear as AI types)
  • WebSocket Connection - (Expanded only) Establish persistent connection for voice conversations
  • Audio Streaming - (Expanded only) Handle bidirectional audio streams for voice mode

6. Contact Form Integration

Connect the existing contact form to automatically launch the chat experience.

  • Form Submission Handler Update - Modify the contact form API route to initialize a chat session upon successful submission
  • Automatic Chat Launch - Replace "Thank you" page with chat interface, pre-loaded with user context
  • Session Management - Create and maintain unique session IDs linking contact form submissions to chat conversations

Phase C: Intelligence Layer (Weeks 5-6)

7. Lead Scoring & Analysis System

Build the AI system that evaluates conversation quality and generates insights.

  • Question Prediction Engine - Implement AI logic that analyzes contact form data (product type, timeline, business stage) to generate 4 contextually relevant suggested questions
  • Analysis Prompt Engineering - Design specialized AI prompts that analyze conversations and extract structured insights
  • Quality Scoring Algorithm - Implement logic to score leads based on engagement, question quality, timeline readiness, and budget indicators
  • Topic Extraction - Use AI to identify and categorize main discussion topics from each conversation
  • Sentiment Analysis - Detect customer enthusiasm, concerns, or hesitation from conversation tone
  • JSON Output Generation - Structure all analysis results into clean, consistent JSON format for dashboard display

8. Smart CTA Logic

Implement intelligent decision-making for when and how to suggest booking calls.

  • High-Quality Lead Detection - Recognize signals indicating a serious, ready-to-buy customer (specific timeline, clear budget, detailed questions)
  • Knowledge Gap Detection - Identify when questions exceed the AI's knowledge base and human expertise is needed
  • CTA Timing Engine - Determine optimal moments in the conversation to suggest booking a call (not too early, not too late)
  • Booking Link Display - Render prominent, attractive call-to-action with calendar booking integration
  • Alternative Paths - Offer different next steps based on lead quality (consultation call, send quote, download resource, etc.)

9. Survey & Feedback System

Capture user satisfaction and feedback at conversation end.

  • Survey Modal Component - Pop-up interface with star rating and optional text feedback field
  • Trigger Logic - Detect conversation end (user closes chat, long inactivity, or explicit goodbye)
  • Data Storage - Save ratings and comments to database linked to the chat session
  • Analytics Aggregation - Calculate average ratings, identify trends, and flag negative feedback

Phase D: Admin Dashboard (Week 7)

10. Sanity Studio Chat Integration

Build custom components for viewing and managing chat data in your admin panel.

  • Chat History Viewer Component - Custom Sanity component displaying full conversation transcripts with clean formatting
  • Lead Analysis Display - Visual cards showing quality scores, topic tags, and AI recommendations at a glance
  • Contact Form Enhancement - Add "View Chat History" button to each contact form submission in the existing table
  • Search & Filter - Enable filtering chats by date, quality score, topics discussed, or feedback rating
  • Export Functionality - Allow exporting chat transcripts as PDF or text for offline review
  • User Management Dashboard - (Expanded only) View all registered users, their activity levels, and conversation histories

11. Analytics Dashboard

Create visual insights and metrics about chat performance and lead quality.

  • Engagement Metrics - Display total chats, average conversation length, messages per chat, and completion rates
  • Lead Quality Distribution - Charts showing breakdown of lead scores and quality levels
  • Topic Trends - Visualize most commonly discussed topics and customer concerns
  • Conversion Tracking - Monitor how many chats result in consultation bookings
  • Satisfaction Scores - Display average ratings, trends over time, and user feedback highlights

Phase E: User Authentication (Expanded Only, Week 8-9)

12. Authentication System

Implement secure user registration and login functionality.

  • Auth Provider Setup - Integrate NextAuth.js or Clerk for secure authentication
  • Automatic Account Creation - System can auto-generate accounts using contact form email/name and send credentials via email
  • Manual Registration Flow - Alternative email/password signup with verification for users who prefer to set their own credentials
  • Login Interface - Secure login page with "Remember Me" and password reset
  • Social Authentication - Optional Google/LinkedIn OAuth integration
  • Session Management - Secure token-based sessions with automatic refresh
  • User Profile Page - Dashboard where users view their information and conversation history

13. Chat History & Persistence

Build the system for users to access and continue previous conversations.

  • History Interface - User-facing page listing all their past conversations with dates and preview snippets
  • Conversation Resume - Allow users to re-open any previous chat and continue where they left off
  • Search Functionality - Let users search through their own chat history
  • Context Restoration - Load full conversation history when resuming, so AI has complete context
  • New Chat Initiation - Allow logged-in users to start new conversations without re-submitting contact forms

Phase F: Testing & Deployment (Week 8 for MVP, Week 10-12 for Expanded)

14. Quality Assurance & Testing

Thoroughly test all functionality to ensure reliability and quality.

  • Conversation Flow Testing - Test dozens of different conversation scenarios and edge cases
  • AI Response Quality Review - Evaluate accuracy, tone, and helpfulness of AI answers
  • Lead Scoring Validation - Verify the analysis system correctly identifies high/low quality leads
  • Browser Compatibility - Test on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and mobile browsers
  • Voice Quality Testing - (Expanded only) Test audio clarity, latency, and reliability across devices
  • Load Testing - Ensure system handles multiple simultaneous conversations
  • Security Audit - Review authentication, data storage, and API security

15. FAQ Content Creation

Develop the knowledge base that powers the AI's responses.

  • FAQ Documentation - Work with Elixderm team to document 30-50 most common questions with detailed, accurate answers
  • Edge Case Responses - Define how AI should handle uncommon or out-of-scope questions
  • Tone & Voice Guidelines - Establish the AI's personality (professional, friendly, consultative)
  • Iterative Refinement - Test with real scenarios and refine responses based on results

16. Deployment & Monitoring

Launch the system to production and set up ongoing monitoring.

  • Production Deployment - Deploy all components to Vercel hosting environment
  • Environment Configuration - Set up production API keys, database connections, and security settings
  • Error Tracking Setup - Implement logging and error monitoring (Sentry or similar)
  • Cost Monitoring - Set up alerts for OpenAI API usage and costs
  • Performance Monitoring - Track response times, uptime, and user experience metrics
  • Training Documentation - Provide admin guide for managing chats, updating FAQs, and interpreting analytics
